# MAHA Growth Loop V1

## Objective
Build a low-cost, self-distributing digital product where customer usage naturally creates a shareable artifact, and sharing unlocks additional product value.

## Working product
**MAHA Business Growth Score** — a free business/web presence diagnostic that produces a public, shareable scorecard.

The product is designed around this loop:

`Create score → Share scorecard → Recipient sees value → Recipient creates own score → New user`

## Benefit loop
- Free user receives one initial scorecard.
- A scorecard has a compact `Powered by MAHA` attribution and a CTA: `Get your own score`.
- Sharing/publishing the scorecard unlocks additional analysis credits.
- Referral activity can unlock higher-value diagnostics instead of cash rewards.
- Paid Pro features are introduced only after usage and distribution are proven.

## V1 principles
1. Start with zero/near-zero infrastructure cost.
2. No paid ads required for validation.
3. Do not require spam or forced messaging.
4. The shared artifact must be useful to the recipient.
5. Rewards are product value, not artificial points.
6. Measure actual activation, shares, referred visitors, referred activations, and revenue.
7. Never claim guaranteed income or viral growth.

## Success gates
- 10 real users
- 5+ published/shared scorecards
- 3+ referred visitors
- 1+ referred activation
- first paid upgrade only after the free loop works
